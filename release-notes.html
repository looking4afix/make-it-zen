Make it Zen 0.8.1

Make it Zen was updated. Here is what changed.

  • Players: in a narrow sidebar the volume slider no longer spills out of the card. It now appears above the speaker button while you hover it.
  • Glance: new setting, Share my sign-in with Glance previews (experimental, on by default). Firefox keeps a site's cookies apart when it is shown inside another site, so a preview can look signed out. Click inside the preview and Firefox may ask to let the site use its cookies; if you allow it, the sign-in is available from then on: a link you click opens signed in, and a click anywhere else reloads the preview once.
  • This page: it opens after every update. You can turn that off in the settings.
  • Firefox for Android is no longer listed as supported: the sidebar, Alt+click and the keyboard shortcuts are desktop features.

0.8.0

  • Glance now opens sites that refuse to be embedded (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and others), including sites that use a service worker.
  • Links clicked inside the Glance preview keep working when the page you are on has a strict security policy.
  • Long titles in the sidebar player scroll only while the pointer is over them and return to the start when it leaves.
  • Removed experimental settings and debug logging.
